Aircraft performance data derived from OpenAP
============================================================
Source:  TU Delft / OpenAP project
URL:     https://github.com/TUDelft-CNS-ATM/openap
Licence: GNU Lesser General Public License, version 3 (LGPL-3.0)

The aircraft weight limits (OEW, MTOW, MLW, MFC), typical seat counts,
engine list, cruise Mach ranges, ceiling altitudes, WRAP kinematic climb
and descent speed/rate bands, and the fuel-flow model coefficients in
src/SkyNetwork.Site/Aircraft/profiles.json are derived from the OpenAP
dataset (aircraft/*.yml, engine/*.yml, kinematic/ WRAP profiles).

Types not covered by OpenAP (SU95, C172, PA28, TBM9) are mapped to the
closest available profile or based on published flight-manual (POH/AFM)
data; the "_map_note" field in each entry identifies the mapping.
